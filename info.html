About FixLink

FixLink connects people with skilled local professionals such as plumbers, electricians, carpenters and cleaners, so finding help is quick and trustworthy.

How it works

Search or pick a service, choose a professional, send a request with photos and details, agree the job, pay securely and leave a review when the work is done.

Become a pro

Create a free professional profile, get verified and start receiving requests. Premium Visibility (₦1,200 per month) is optional and puts your profile higher in your category.

Help centre

Questions about a booking, payment or account? Start with the FAQs below, or contact us.

FAQs

What fees do customers pay? A 30% FixLink usage fee is added to the professional's job amount. The full total is shown before you pay.

What do professionals pay? Standard professionals have a 10% platform deduction. Premium professionals keep 100% of the job amount.

How are professionals verified? Through ID and skill review before they receive the verified badge.

Safety

Check the professional's name, photo and verified badge before letting them in. Keep payments and chats inside FixLink, and report anything that feels wrong.
